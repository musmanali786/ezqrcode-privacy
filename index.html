Privacy Policy

QR Code Designer

Published by Hayat Tech · Effective October 14, 2026

QR Code Designer ("the app") is a QR code generator and scanner made by Hayat Tech. This page explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and how you can delete it. The app works fully offline for generating, designing, scanning, and saving QR codes — an account is only needed if you want your library synced across devices.

1 Data we collect

DataWhy we collect itWhere it's stored
Email addressCreating and signing in to your account (email/password or Google Sign-In)Supabase (our backend provider)
QR codes you create or saveSo your library is available in the app, and across devices if you're signed inOn your device always; also on Supabase if you're signed in

We don't collect your name, phone number, contacts, or precise location, and we don't read the contents of your photo library beyond the single image you choose to pick.

2 Advertising

Free-tier use of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. Where required by law (including the EU, UK, and Switzerland), you'll be shown a consent screen before any personalized ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time from AdMob's privacy options. You can remove ads entirely with the in-app "Remove Ads" subscription.

3 Third-party services

  • Supabase — authentication and cloud storage for your synced QR library.
  • Google AdMob — ad serving and consent management for free-tier users.
  • Google Play Billing — processes the ad-free subscription purchase.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in method, if you choose it instead of email/password.

We don't sell your data, and we don't share it with anyone beyond the service providers above, each acting under their own privacy terms, solely to provide the functionality listed in this policy.

4 Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Profile → Delete Account & Data. For signed-in accounts, this permanently deletes your account and every QR code saved to the cloud. For guest use, it clears everything saved on your device. This cannot be undone.

You can also just uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally on your device.

5 Children's privacy

QR Code Designer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
